Trends in Mental Health by Sexual Orientation: Evidence from the 2013-2024 National Health Interview Survey

Gilbert Gonzales, Dalton Malmin, Christopher Julian. Submitted.

Abstract

Using 2013–2024 National Health Interview Survey data, we examined trends in mental health symptoms, medication use, and cost-related barriers to mental health care by sexual orientation. Gay and lesbian adults continued to experience disparities relative to heterosexual adults, while bisexual adults reported substantial increases in anxiety and unmet mental health care due to cost. Medication use for anxiety and depression increased among both groups.

Health Insurance Arrangements among Same-Sex and Different-Sex Couples

Dalton Malmin, Gilbert Gonzales, Christopher Julian.

Abstract

Using pooled 2019, 2021, 2023, and 2025 Current Population Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement data, we examine health insurance arrangements among same-sex and different-sex couples with both partners aged 26–64. We estimate weighted percentages comparing male–male, female–female, and different-sex couples on coverage through a partner’s employer-sponsored plan, other insurance arrangements, and uninsured status.
